Partner · Strategy

Edwina Pearse

Corporate strategy, board advisory, and the hard yes/no calls most firms are too polite to name.

Edwina spent fifteen years inside boardrooms — first as a strategy consultant, then as a chief of staff to two very different CEOs — before deciding she would rather advise a handful of companies properly than a hundred at arm's length.

She reads a business the way an editor reads a manuscript: quickly, generously, and with a sharp eye for the sentence that's doing all the work. Warm in the room, ruthless on the page. She'll tell you the thing your board is too polite to.

Partner · Operations

Drew Pearse

Operating models, margin recovery, and the quiet fixes that give a founder their weekends back.

Drew came up through operations the hard way — a warehouse floor, then a national logistics rollout, then a decade untangling the businesses that grew faster than their systems. He is calm, methodical, and mildly obsessed with checklists.

He is the person you want when a plan has to actually happen. He'll map the messy middle nobody wants to own, then hand it back to your team documented well enough that they never need to call us again — which, he insists, is the point.
